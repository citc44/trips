Voylo Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026

Voylo ("we", "our", "the app") lets a group of people traveling together see each other's live location during a shared trip ("Voyage") and view a recap afterward. This policy explains what information Voylo collects, why, and how it's handled.

Information we collect

Account information. Your email address, used solely to sign you in via a one-time passcode (OTP). We don't use passwords.

Location data. While you are an active participant in a Voyage and have granted location permission, Voylo collects your device's location so it can be shown live to other members of that same Voyage. Location collection stops when you leave or end the Voyage, or revoke permission in your device settings.

Voyage and trip content. Information you or your group create within a Voyage (e.g. voyage name, membership, join codes) is stored to power the shared map and trip recap.

Diagnostic information. We may collect basic crash and error reports to help us fix bugs. These reports do not include your precise location or voyage content.

How we use information

  • To authenticate you and maintain your session
  • To show your live location to, and show you the live location of, other members of a Voyage you're actively participating in
  • To generate a shareable recap of a completed Voyage for its members
  • To diagnose and fix technical problems

Who can see your information

Your location is visible only to other members of the same active Voyage — never to the public, and never to members of a different Voyage. We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers.

Third-party services

Voylo relies on the following service providers to operate:

  • Supabase — authentication, database, and real-time location sync
  • Resend — delivery of sign-in emails
  • Mapbox — map rendering

These providers process data only as necessary to provide their service to Voylo and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes.

Data retention

Live location data is retained only for the duration needed to support the active Voyage and its recap. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us below.

Children's privacy

Voylo is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
