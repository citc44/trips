Direction C — "Burst & Settle"

Motion: tapping the marker fires a one-shot amber ring that radiates outward from the marker itself and fades (distinct from the marker's own continuous live-pulse — this one fires once, on tap). The card springs up from behind the marker with a soft overshoot-then-settle, landing with a little weight to it. Closing drops the card back down and shrinks it away quickly. Deliberately reuses the same "radiating ring" motion vocabulary already established for the Destination Picker → Live Map "cut to gameplay" flash, so this reads as a small sibling of that bigger moment rather than an unrelated new idea.

Layout: stacked rows like Direction A, but each stat icon sits on a solid, bold color chip (teal / amber) rather than a soft tint — reads more like a game token/badge than a subtle utility icon.

Try it: tap "Open Card", then "Close", then switch between Meera/Yourself. Tap the copy icon to see the checkmark morph. The filled blue arrow beside it is the new "Get Directions" control (added per user request) — opens the OS maps app with driving directions to that Voyager. Other-Voyager cards only; omitted on your own card, same rationale as distance-from-you.
