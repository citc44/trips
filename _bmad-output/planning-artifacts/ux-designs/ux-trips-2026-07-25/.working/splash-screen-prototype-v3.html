"The Thread" — apart, connected, and collecting as you go

Back on the bright Horizon-Drift sky gradient, not the dark navy from round 2. Three Voyagers appear separately — not converging — then a single amber thread draws itself live between them, exactly like a road being traced on the map. Small sparks pop in along the thread as it passes them: moments being collected in real time. It resolves into the Voylo wordmark sitting under the connected trio, echoing the brand line "every journey tells a story, and Voylo makes sure you never miss it."
