Direction B — "Card Deal"

Motion: the card enters like a trading card being dealt face-up off the marker — starts tilted back and scaled down (as if lying flat behind the marker), then rotates and rises into place with a slight overshoot, landing flush. A one-shot diagonal shine sweeps across the card surface right as it lands, a small "foil card" flourish. Closing tips the card back down and away, mirroring the deal in reverse but faster.

Layout: a HUD scoreboard stat-pair — distance-from-you and distance-from-destination sit side by side under a divider, in the numeral-mono typeface already reserved for stats elsewhere in Wayfinder (Space Mono / stat-numeral-sm) — leans hardest into the "game HUD" read of the three directions.

Try it: tap "Open Card", then "Close", then switch between Meera/Yourself. Tap the copy icon to see the checkmark morph. The filled blue arrow beside it is the new "Get Directions" control (added per user request) — opens the OS maps app with driving directions to that Voyager. Other-Voyager cards only; omitted on your own card, same rationale as distance-from-you.
