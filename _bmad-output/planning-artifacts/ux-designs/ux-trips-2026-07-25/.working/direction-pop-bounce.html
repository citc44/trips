Direction A — "Pop & Bounce"

Motion: tapping a marker triggers a small character-hop on the avatar itself, then the card pops up with an elastic overshoot (scales past 100% before settling) — a spring, not a fade. A quick burst of amber sparks radiates from behind the card at the same moment, echoing the pulse-ring language already on the map marker. Closing is a fast, snappy shrink-away (~180ms) rather than a mirrored open — the "video game" read comes from that asymmetry: satisfying to open, quick to dismiss.

Layout: stacked rows, each stat with its own small icon chip (teal chip for distance-from-you, amber chip for distance-from-destination) — scannable top-to-bottom, closest to the existing card's current shape.

Try it: tap "Open Card", then "Close", then switch between Meera/Yourself. Tap the copy icon to see the checkmark morph. The filled blue arrow beside it is the new "Get Directions" control (added per user request) — opens the OS maps app with driving directions to that Voyager. Other-Voyager cards only; omitted on your own card, same rationale as distance-from-you.
