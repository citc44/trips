Direction B — "Player Constellation"

Idea: instead of a road/journey motif, the crew's own player colors carry the whole deck — the same coral/teal/amber (and beyond) already tied to each Voyager everywhere else in the app. The story being told is "look who was with you," not "look where you went."

Trigger screen: a light surface-secondary canvas (deliberately calmer than a full-bleed hero — this direction earns its excitement through the crew converging, not through color intensity) with three player-color dots gently orbiting a center point, like a constellation still forming.

Opening/who-joined card: the same dots sweep in from off-screen with comet trails (reusing map-marker's existing trail mechanic) and converge — literally dramatizing "everyone arrived."

Finale: the dots settle into a ring around the closing message, with a soft amber burst (reusing marker-peek-card's sparkBurst token) — a "group hug" read rather than Direction A's "completed road."

Constraint check: no new hues (uses only already-approved player-color tokens), no transparency/blur, no new dark surface.
