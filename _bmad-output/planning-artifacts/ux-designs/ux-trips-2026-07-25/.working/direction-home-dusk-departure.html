Direction F — "Dusk Departure"

Builds on Direction B by pushing richness through light and color instead of more graphic elements: the whole sky slowly breathes through a warm golden-hour gradient (14s cycle), a soft amber sun pulses gently, and a rare, tasteful shooting-star spark crosses the sky every ~7s -- "adventure awaits" read cinematically rather than literally. The road itself stays simpler (single plane, no parallax) so the atmosphere carries the richness, not more objects on screen.

Richer because: mood and light do the emotional work -- closest in spirit to a movie-trailer establishing shot, which echoes the brainstorming session's own "Trip Trailer Method" framing for the brand's voice.

Tradeoff to weigh: warm amber/coral tones lean slightly warmer than Wayfinder's usual blue-forward palette -- worth deciding if that's a welcome seasonal/mood accent for this one screen or a bigger palette question.
