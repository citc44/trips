Direction C — "Gathering Dots"

Concept: the quietest, most conceptual of the three. Rather than a road motif, this borrows the Splash sequence's own established visual language -- three soft, glowing player-color orbs (coral/teal/amber, the same three colors app-icon and splash-thread already use) drifting slowly in the background, apart but never quite meeting. It's a wordless echo of the brand's first-principle idea: "let people experience a journey together, even when physically apart" -- the dots aren't connected yet because the Voyage hasn't started yet.

Copy: same tagline placement as Direction A, in the quiet middle zone.

Motion: each dot drifts on its own slow, independent loop (9-11s, gentle ease-in-out) -- ambient and continuous, never converging or bursting (that payoff is reserved for Splash's own "Thread" sequence and the "cut to gameplay" transition). Freezes to a static frame under Reduce Motion.

Tradeoff to weigh: most subtle of the three -- reads as sophisticated/branded on close inspection, but is the lightest-touch option if what you want is something more immediately, obviously "road trip."
