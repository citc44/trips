Direction C — "Ignition Bloom"

Idea: the most maximalist of the three. Leans hardest into Wayfinder's existing full-bleed accent-primary "biggest emotional beat" treatment (same register as Voyage Intro/Join Invitation) and punctuates every transition with a theatrical amber burst — a bigger cousin of marker-peek-card's existing spark-burst micro-interaction, scaled up to be the star rather than a garnish.

Trigger screen: a pulsing amber "ignition" core at center — literally the button-ignition idea (press → physical response) turned into ambient motion, building anticipation before the tap.

Opening card: the tap itself triggers an amber spark-burst radiating outward as the destination name resolves into view — cause and effect, not just decoration.

Finale: a brief warm amber wash crosses the whole screen like a camera flash/bloom before settling on the closing message — the most "look what we made" theatrical of the three, closest to a highlight-reel sting.

Constraint check: reuses only accent-primary/accent-amber (no new hues), no transparency/blur, no new dark surface — the wash is a solid color at partial keyframe opacity, not a persistent translucent layer.
