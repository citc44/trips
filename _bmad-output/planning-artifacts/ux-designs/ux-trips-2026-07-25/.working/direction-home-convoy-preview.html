Direction E — "Convoy Preview"

Builds on Direction B by populating the road with tiny map-marker-style dots in the exact same player-color palette Live Map itself uses (teal/coral/amber) -- gently bobbing in place up the road. The idea: Home isn't just showing you a generic road, it's previewing the actual game-world map vocabulary you'll see the instant you start a Voyage. It's illustrative only here (no real data, no names) -- but it plants the "you're about to join others on a living map" idea before you've even tapped anything.

Richer because: it's not just atmosphere, it's a literal, wordless preview of the app's core mechanic (a shared live map) -- ties Home's fantasy directly to what actually happens next, rather than a mood-only visual.

Tradeoff to weigh: illustrative dots with no real people could feel slightly staged/misleading on a completely solo first launch (no crew has joined yet) -- worth deciding whether that's a fun tease or a small trust wrinkle.
