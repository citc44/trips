Voylo · Icon exploration, round 2 · .working/

Same palette, much more dimension and drama

Round 1 was flat color-block silhouettes — read as safe/corporate. This round leans into depth, glow, motion streaks, and gradient light instead of flat fills, closer to how a game or arcade-style app icon earns attention on a crowded home screen. Still built only from Wayfinder tokens — nothing new introduced, just used with more dimensionality.

Concept E

Convergence Burst

Round 1's dots, given impact. Radial light behind them, motion streaks trailing off each orb like they just rocketed in, and inset shading so each dot reads as a lit sphere, not a flat circle. Same "friends converging" idea, now with actual velocity and light instead of static geometry.

Concept F

Road Portal

The most "wow, what is that" of the four. A radiant vanishing-point — sunrise amber core blooming into Wayfinder blue, then night at the edges — with road lines rushing toward the viewer instead of sitting flat. Reads like a portal into the trip, not a map. Highest-risk, highest-reward option.

Concept G

Voyager Badge

Leans hardest into "reads as entertainment, not a utility" — a game achievement-badge treatment (diagonal teal-to-blue gradient, embossed ring, glossy amber star) instead of anything map-shaped at all. Closest in spirit to a level-complete or trophy icon; a deliberate swing away from navigation-app visual cliché.

Concept H

Speed Mark

Purely abstract, purely velocity. No pin, no road, no compass — three white speed-bars racing diagonally out of a blue-to-coral gradient, with a single amber accent stroke standing in for "your Voyage." Most legible at tiny sizes of all eight concepts so far since it's built from bars, not fine linework.
