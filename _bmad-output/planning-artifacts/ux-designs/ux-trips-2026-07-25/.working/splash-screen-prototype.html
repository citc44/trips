"Ignition" — a launch sequence, not a logo screen

Purely animated, no tap required — it plays once, automatically, then hands off to Home. Built on top of the Horizon Drift ambient-footer language already locked into DESIGN.md, scaled up to fill the whole splash instead of just a footer strip. The three dots use the same player colors as the live map markers, so the very first thing you see already speaks the app's visual language.

Accessibility Floor: when the OS reduce-motion setting is on, the flight paths, spring bounce, idle bob, and continuous road-scroll are all disabled. Everything appears already-settled with one soft 400ms crossfade — same end state, no motion in transit. Toggle the checkbox above to preview that fallback.
  1. 0–200ms — solid accent-primary field, phone already "on."
  2. 200–1140ms — three player-colored dots fly in from off-frame and spring-settle into a loose cluster.
  3. 1000–1750ms — horizon strip rises from the bottom: sky gradient, sun glow, then the dashed amber centerline begins scrolling left, continuously, for the rest of the sequence.
  4. 1550–2050ms — "Voylo" wordmark rises into place under the dot cluster, tagline fades in after.
  5. 2050ms+ — idle hold: dots bob gently, road keeps drifting, until the app is ready and cross-fades to Home.
