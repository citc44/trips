Direction D — "Living Road" (parallax depth)

Builds on Direction B by adding real depth instead of one flat road plane: distant hills drift slowly along the horizon, roadside markers rush past and scale up as they pass (classic parallax speed-lines trick), and the centerline advances faster than before. The combined effect reads as genuine forward motion, not just a decorated static scene -- closer to what "stylized game-world" actually implies from the original brainstorming session (Road Rash / racing-HUD reference).

Richer because: three independent motion layers at three different speeds, not one. This is the most technically involved of the richer set -- worth weighing implementation cost against the payoff during Story 4.8.
