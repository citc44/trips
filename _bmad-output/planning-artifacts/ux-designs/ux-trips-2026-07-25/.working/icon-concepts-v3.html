Apart, but one thread — and the trip leaves marks

Grounded in what you actually described: separate cars, one shared story, and the app quietly collecting moments as you go. All three below keep the three Voyager dots apart, not merged, joined by a single road-like thread carrying small collected-moment marks — different only in how literal the "road" reads.

Concept I

The Thread (dashed road)

Uses the app's own map-road-centerline dash pattern as the connecting thread — literally the same visual language as the live map. Two tiny white dots along the path stand in for collected moments banked along the way.

Concept J

The Thread (soft ribbon)

Same idea, softer execution — a solid ribbon instead of a dashed road, amber sparks (not white dots) marking the collected moments so they read as small "finds" rather than waypoints. Friendlier, less map-utility, more storybook.

Concept K

The Unfinished Loop

The thread almost closes into a loop but deliberately doesn't — "the story isn't finished yet." Most abstract of the three; reads as a single continuous journey rather than three fixed points, at slight cost to legibility at the smallest sizes (favicon/notification).
