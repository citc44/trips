Four directions, same Wayfinder identity

None of these are picked yet — this board exists so you can react. All four draw only from tokens already locked in DESIGN.md (accent-primary blue, the three most-used player colors, accent-amber). Shown at 128px (roughly true iOS home-screen size) and in context next to placeholder apps, since an icon that reads well at full size can still go illegible at 60px.

Concept A

Convergent Voyagers

Reuses the exact visual grammar of the live-map markers — the one asset every Voyager already recognizes from inside the app — shrunk to icon scale. Three player-colored dots drifting toward a shared center reads as "everyone converging on one trip" before the app even opens.

Concept B

The Road Pin

A pin, but not a static "you are here" marker — the dashed amber trail behind it is the same centerline language as the map's roads and the Horizon Drift footer, so the icon itself implies motion: a journey already in progress, not a place you're stuck at.

Concept C

Wayfinder Compass-Pin

The most literal nod to the "Wayfinder" identity itself — a compass needle fused with the pin shape from Concept B. Reads as more serious/utility than A or B; worth including as the control since it's the direction closest to a generic "maps app" icon, which the product deliberately doesn't want to feel like.

Concept D

The V Road Monogram

Breaks from the map/pin metaphor entirely: a bold "V" wordmark whose left stroke carries a dashed amber centerline, so the road motif survives as a texture rather than a literal object. Most legible of the four at very small sizes (browser favicon, notification icon) since it has the fewest discrete shapes.
