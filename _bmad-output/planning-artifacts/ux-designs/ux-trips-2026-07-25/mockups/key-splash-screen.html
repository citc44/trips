Apart, connected, and collecting as you go

Plays once, automatically, on cold launch — no tap required, no skip control. Three Voyagers appear separately, an amber thread draws itself live between them (the same road-centerline language as the live map), small sparks pop in along it as it passes — moments being collected in real time — then resolves into the Voylo wordmark and the brand line. On Wayfinder's bright sky-gradient register, matching the rest of the system.

Voylo
Every journey tells a story.
Accessibility Floor: reduce-motion shows the fully-connected end state immediately (thread already drawn, sparks already settled) with one soft crossfade in — no drawing animation, no pop, no ripple. Same rule as every other Motion & Transitions entry in EXPERIENCE.md.
  1. 0–520ms — the three Voyager dots (player-teal, player-coral, player-amber) pop in separately, at a distance from each other.
  2. 620–1770ms — an accent-amber thread draws itself live (SVG stroke-dashoffset reveal), tracing a path between all three.
  3. 1100–1720ms — three spark marks pop in along the thread as it passes them.
  4. 1760–2460ms — a soft accent-primary ring ripples out from the last connection point.
  5. 1900–2600ms — the Voylo wordmark rises in under the connected trio; "Every journey tells a story." fades in after.
  6. 2400ms+ — idle hold: dots breathe gently, sparks twinkle, until the app is ready and cross-fades to whatever the launch destination is (Home, OTP Entry, or a resumed Live Map).
