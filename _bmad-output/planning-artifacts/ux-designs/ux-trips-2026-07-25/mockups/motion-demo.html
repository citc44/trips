What's happening

Home → Voyage Intro → Destination Picker

Standard push: incoming screen slides in 28px + fades in, outgoing slides out 20px + fades out. 360ms, cubic-bezier(.22,.85,.35,1). Back reverses the direction.

Destination Picker → Live Map

The one bigger moment: a solid-blue flash pulses and scales up (500ms) while the destination name appears, then Live Map reveals underneath with a quick scale-down-to-rest pop (420ms, delayed to land after the flash). This is the "cut to gameplay" beat EXPERIENCE.md already called for but never specified.

Hamburger → Action Drawer

Drawer slides in from the right (280ms) while a scrim fades in behind it. Tapping the scrim or the close button reverses both.

Buttons

Primary CTA presses down 3px (mimics the ignition button's shadow going flat); small icon buttons (back arrow, hamburger, drawer close) scale to 90% on press. Tactile, not decorative.

Accessibility

Every animation above is disabled under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce — screens still change, just without the motion.
