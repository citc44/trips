- (technique) started Utopia vs Dystopia Split-Screen
- (idea by user) App name: Voylo. Utopia brochure line: 'Send me your Voylo' becomes a verb, like Strava/Wordle. Tagline: 'Every journey tells a story. We make sure you never miss it.'
- (idea by user) Dystopia headline: 'Voylo spent years building highlight videos. Users just wanted to know where everyone was.' - risk is feature-sequencing/over-polish, not privacy scandal - validates locking the core MVP before the fun layer
- (insight) SYNTHESIS: user's key takeaway = the brand (Voylo, 'send me your Voylo') is the throughline. Connections: brand promise is only earned if locked MVP (presence+stats) is rock solid first (dystopia = direct threat to brand line); badges/Hall of Fame-Shame/planned-vs-actual stat ARE the content of 'your Voylo' - the app produces a shareable artifact, not just a service; game-world map + thematic sound are the sensory costume of the adventurous brand; personality badges double as the viral growth loop (seeing a friend's Voylo drives signup). Chosen direction: ship the locked MVP (start/join journey, live map, pace stats) first, prove it, then layer F1 live-mode features, highlight reels, and badges on top.
